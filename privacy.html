Privacy policy.

Last updated: November 8, 2026. This policy explains what information may be processed when you visit the MCX Digital website or contact us through the site.

1. Information you submit

If you use the contact form, you may be asked to provide your name, email address, business or organization name (optional), and a message. Please do not submit passwords, payment details, confidential client information, or other sensitive personal information.

Contact form submissions are sent through Web3Forms, a third-party form processing service, so your inquiry can be delivered to MCX Digital. Web3Forms may process the information you enter and technical information associated with the submission, such as your IP address and browser details, including for service operation and spam prevention. Web3Forms' published privacy policy states that submission data may be retained for up to three years, depending on the applicable plan and form settings, and describes its infrastructure and service providers. Review the Web3Forms Privacy Policy for current details.

MCX Digital receives inquiries at matthew@mcxdigital.net. Messages and related correspondence may be retained in the email account and business records for as long as reasonably needed to respond, follow up, and maintain business records.

2. Website hosting and technical information

This website is hosted and delivered using Cloudflare services. In providing hosting, DNS, security, and content delivery, Cloudflare may process technical information such as IP addresses, request details, and other network or device metadata. For more information, see Cloudflare's Privacy Policy.

3. Fonts and external links

This website loads fonts from Google Fonts. When your browser requests those font files, Google may receive your IP address and technical information needed to deliver the fonts. See Google's Privacy Policy and the Google Fonts privacy information.

The website also contains links to third-party services, including Upwork and Web3Forms. If you follow an external link, that service's own privacy policy and terms apply to your use of it.

4. Analytics, advertising, and cookies

MCX Digital does not currently use Google Analytics, advertising pixels, or other intentionally installed website analytics or advertising-tracking tools on this site. The hosting and security services used to operate the website may process technical information or use essential cookies or similar technologies where needed for security and service functionality.

5. How to contact MCX Digital about privacy

If you have a question about this policy or want to ask about information you submitted through the website, contact MCX Digital at matthew@mcxdigital.net. Please describe your request without including passwords or other sensitive information.

6. Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated when the website, its service providers, or its information practices change. The latest version will be published on this page with its updated date.

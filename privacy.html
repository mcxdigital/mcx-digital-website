Privacy policy.

This page explains how the website is currently intended to operate. It will be updated if contact forms, analytics, or additional services are introduced.

This is a working draft, not legal advice. It describes the website in its current setup. Review it before launch and update it if the site begins collecting information or uses additional services.

1. Information submitted through this website

The contact form currently displayed on this website is inactive. It does not submit or store the information typed into it. Please do not enter personal or business information into the inactive form. If you contact MCX Digital through another method, information you choose to provide may be used to respond to your inquiry and discuss requested services.

2. Hosting and technical information

This website is hosted using Cloudflare. Like most website hosting providers, Cloudflare may process technical information such as IP addresses, browser details, and request logs to deliver, protect, and maintain the service. Refer to Cloudflare's privacy documentation for details about its handling of data.

3. Fonts and external links

The site loads fonts from Google Fonts, which may involve a connection between your browser and Google. The website may also link to third-party services, including Upwork or a future customer portal. Those services operate under their own privacy policies and terms.

4. Analytics and tracking

No website analytics or advertising-tracking tools have been intentionally added to the current site. This statement will need to be revisited if such tools are introduced.

5. Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated as the website and services change. The updated version will be posted on this page.

6. Contact

Business contact details will be published here before the website launches to customers.

01 The $750 website package covers up to five standard informational pages and the inclusions listed above. It does not include e-commerce, custom applications, complex animations or coding, complex booking or membership systems, bespoke human-written copywriting, or unlimited revisions and support. The client may provide specific wording; standard website copy is AI-assisted and based on client-provided information. One year of hosting on an MCX Digital-selected plan is included. New domain registration is covered up to $15; if you already own a domain, we can connect it to the website, but using an existing domain does not reduce the package price or create a credit. Domain and hosting renewals after the first year are separate and paid by the client. One optional app integration is available for $100, covering one straightforward connection to a supported third-party app using its standard integration or embed features. Paid app subscriptions, custom API development, complex automation, and third-party troubleshooting are not included and may require a separate quote. Additional pages, features, content work, and requests outside the agreed scope are quoted separately. Year-one maintenance is limited to two hours total for minor fixes and small content corrections. Delivery is typically 7–10 business days after required materials are received, with timing dependent on client feedback and approvals.

02 The $350 CRM package is intended for a small business moving from paper records or spreadsheets into one basic lead and sales management system. The initial scope includes one business, one platform, one pipeline of up to 8 stages, up to 10 custom fields, up to 3 users, one organized spreadsheet import of up to 250 records, one basic follow-up process, and a walkthrough of up to 30 minutes. The $650 Jobber option is an alternative setup using Jobber in place of the standard CRM platform, with additional configuration for service businesses. CRM subscriptions are purchased and paid for directly by the client, and the account remains under the client’s ownership. The client supplies business information and reasonably organized contact data. Extensive data cleanup, deduplication, manual data entry, large migrations, multiple pipelines, complex automation, custom integrations, API development, scheduling/dispatch configuration beyond the defined Jobber setup, and ongoing administration are not included. Existing CRM cleanup or migration projects are assessed and quoted separately. Requirements outside the agreed scope may require a separate quote. Final inclusions and delivery timing will be confirmed before work begins.

03 The $350 standard Business Systems & Workflow Setup covers one small business using two primary products: Google Workspace Business Starter and Slack Pro, with up to 3 user accounts in each platform. Microsoft 365 may replace Google Workspace when it better suits the business; it is not configured alongside Google Workspace in the standard package. Implementation includes one domain's basic business email configuration; basic Drive folder structure and sharing permissions; basic Docs, Sheets, and Calendar setup; one Slack workspace with up to 5 channels; basic member roles, channel organization, and notifications; one standard Google Drive-to-Slack integration; testing; and one handoff session of up to 30 minutes. The client must purchase and maintain the lowest appropriate paid business subscription tier for each product being implemented before work begins. The recommended tiers are Google Workspace Business Starter and Slack Pro. Software subscriptions, domain registration or renewal, taxes, and other third-party fees are not included in the $350 service price and are paid directly by the client; accounts remain client-owned. The package covers new account configuration, not recovery or repair of existing accounts, existing email or file migration, large-scale data transfer, complex file restructuring, complex Slack workflows, multiple or custom integrations, advanced security or compliance policies, custom automation or API development, or ongoing administration, training, or help desk support. The client supplies business information, domain access, and any files or content to be organized. Additional users, products, migrations, or work beyond this defined scope require a separate quote. The internal delivery target is approximately 5 hours; if discovery indicates the work will exceed the defined scope or 6 hours, the scope must be revised or additional work quoted before proceeding. Final requirements and timing are confirmed before work begins.

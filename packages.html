01 Website · $750. Includes up to 5 standard pages, 2 consolidated revision rounds, and the listed features. No CMS or self-service editor, e-commerce, custom applications, complex booking or membership features, or unlimited revisions. The client owns the finished website and may move it to another provider. A new domain registration up to $15 and the first year of hosting are included; the domain is registered in the client's name. Existing domains can be connected, with no package-price credit. Renewal pricing for hosting or domain services will be provided before renewal. Two hours of minor year-one maintenance are included and may be used as needed by submitting a support ticket. Additional work is billed at MCX Digital's standard retail rate. One optional straightforward app integration is $100; custom API work and complex automation are quoted separately. Typical delivery is 7–10 business days after required materials are received.

02 CRM · $350 standard / $650 Jobber. The standard setup is for one business moving from paper or spreadsheets into one basic CRM: one platform (Pipedrive recommended), one pipeline of up to 8 stages, 10 custom fields, 3 users, one reasonably organized spreadsheet import of up to 250 records, a basic follow-up process using tasks or reminders, and a walkthrough of up to 30 minutes. GoHighLevel, HubSpot, Zoho CRM, and Salesforce are supported; Salesforce work is limited to a basic setup using the appropriate entry-level product tier, with final scope confirmed first. The $650 Jobber option is an alternative to a traditional CRM and covers a basic setup for a service business, including core customer, quote, job, scheduling, and invoicing configuration. The client owns the account and pays software fees directly. Data cleanup, deduplication, manual entry, large migrations, complex automation, custom integrations, and ongoing administration are not included. Existing CRM migrations and work beyond this scope are quoted separately.

03 Business systems · $350. Covers one small business, up to 3 team members with accounts in each of two products, and a basic setup of Google Workspace Business Starter plus Slack Pro. Microsoft 365 may replace Google Workspace when appropriate; the standard package does not configure both. Includes new business email configuration for one domain, basic shared file and document organization, calendar setup, one Slack workspace with up to 5 channels, basic roles and notifications, one standard Google Drive- or OneDrive-to-Slack integration where supported, testing, and a 30-minute handoff. This is new configuration, not email or file migration. The client purchases and owns the accounts and pays all software, domain, tax, and third-party fees directly; paid business subscriptions are required before work begins. Complex migrations, custom integrations or automation, advanced security/compliance work, and ongoing administration or help-desk support are excluded. The target is approximately 5 hours; if the work is likely to exceed the defined scope or 6 hours, we will agree on a revised scope or separate quote before proceeding.
